# ACME Airlines IRROPS (`v0.2.0`) Release Notes

- **Release:** `v0.2.0` (Wave-2 Live Engine, Guardrail, API & UI Hardening)
- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Commit Range:** `d706e41`..`f018ff0` (70 commits merged on 2026-10-15 and 2026-10-16 after `v0.1.0`'s last entry `d706e41`)
- **Core Governance Rule:** **"Two Goals, One Guardrail"** — Customer recovery (`acme-customer-agent`) strictly outranks commercial revenue (`acme-sales-agent`).

---

## 1. Executive Summary

`v0.2.0` captures all 70 commits merged to `main` after the `v0.1.0` cutoff (`d706e41`, [#317](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/317)). Following live end-to-end verification against the IAP-protected Cloud Run service (`acme-irrops-demo`) and the deployed Vertex AI Agent Engines (`acme-customer-agent` and `acme-sales-agent`), this release closes gaps across six areas:

1. **Guardrail, Policy & Customer Recovery:** Prevents settled/invalidated offer revival on stale `Accept` taps (`#340`), checks `journey_mismatch` in Guardrail Rule 6 and suppresses phantom hotel vouchers in `customer/skills.py` when `rec.hotel is None` (`#430`), suppresses non-discounted or negative-margin quotes when `cost_floor >= standard_upgrade_fare` and syncs `current_flight` across replanning (`#446`), enforces deterministic vetoes on LLM-classified `Acknowledge` turns (`#421`), records `template_fallback("resolution")` on stale outreach facts (`#422`), completes ANAC 400 `DOC-05..08` handling (`#324`, `#396`), threads `World.mct_minutes` through rebooking (`#414`), releases held DCS seats on post-hold conversion failure (`#354`, `#380`, `#381`), and suppresses Sales when open requests or `needs_human` escalations exist (`#325`, `#392`, `#398`, `#416`).
2. **Vertex AI Agent Engines, `SharedState` & Replay Tape:** Hardens SQLite `SharedState` with WAL + `busy_timeout` (`#288`), persists the `RecordingLlm` replay tape inside the shared snapshot (`#279`), bounds `TapeLlm.entries` to `MAX_ENTRIES = 32` and preserves parse entries across manual replies (`#429`), ticks the runtime on `journey_events` and `whatsapp_messages` polls (`#395`), restores the live clock on reset/manual trigger (`#321`), refreshes `self.llm_mode = validate_env()` inside `_set_up()` after `cloudpickle` unpickling (`#440`), pins transitive `ENGINE_REQUIREMENTS` to `uv.lock` (`#330`, `#367`), caches compiled MCP tool output schemas (`#438`), and enforces 11-field `STRUCTURAL` + `seat_reason` rebuild parity across all 6 presets (`#423`, `#428`).
3. **API, SSE Streaming & Security Fencing:** Bounds `/journeys/{id}/events` SSE streams to 60 s with 3 s keep-alives, `Last-Event-ID` resume, and disconnect detection (`#305`), translates engine RPC failures into typed `429`/`503` responses with `Retry-After` (`#306`, `#335`), catches up pending Sales ticks and guards in-flight polls via `_stir_seq` (`#322`, `#345`, `#351`, `#356`, `#372`, `#374`, `#379`), preserves invalidated offers that were already sent (`#339`), and hardens `/a2a` Bearer token validation and `front.py`/`nginx.conf` path normalization (`#329`, `#357`, `#360`, `#378`).
4. **Operator Console, `DemoPanel` & WhatsApp Frontend:** Surfaces guardrail/suppression reason labels, seat courtesy labels, verification checks, rule reasons, and structured event payloads (`#323`, `#403`, `#413`, `#432`, `#442`, `#443`), synchronizes `DemoPanel` and `ChatView` across resets, `429`/`503` cooldowns, and `503 StepApplied` recoveries (`#328`, `#348`, `#364`, `#366`, `#372`, `#386`), and ensures invalidated or `409` offers never display `Accept` as chosen (`#339`, `#402`, `#431`).
5. **Telemetry, Cloud Trace & Cloud Monitoring:** Drops `otel.sdk.*` self-metrics, adds unique exporter IDs (`add_unique_identifier=True`), configures sub-second rebuild duration buckets, seeds and counts engine 5xx errors (`#337`, `#344`), counts `acme.offers{status="accepted"}` only on `upgrade_confirmed` (`#363`), and preserves `service.name` in Cloud Trace via `resource_regex="service\\.name"` (`#400`).
6. **Documentation, CI, Supply Chain & Scion Factory:** Consolidates documentation under the two-folder rule (`docs/product/` and `docs/scion/`, `#285`, `#286`, `#389`, `#411`, `#420`), records audit and live deployment proofs (`#283`, `#394`), tightens Dependabot/Trivy/Observatory workflow triggers (`#331`, `#341`, `#359`), and upgrades Scion factory orchestration (`#326`, `#393`).

## 2. Guardrail, Policy & Customer Recovery (`guardrail/policy`)

- **Guardrail Rule 5 — No Revival of Settled or Invalidated Offers (`#340`, [#350](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/350) `81a2d7b`):** Restricts self-exclusion in `guardrail_offer.evaluate` Rule 5 to `_LIVE_STATUSES = frozenset({"proposed", "approved", "sent"})` so a stale `Accept` tap on an `invalidated`, `declined`, or `accepted` offer is rejected with `offer_already_made`. Verified live on `acme-irrops-demo-00227-h4f`.
- **Guardrail Rule 6 `journey_mismatch` & No Phantom Hotel Voucher on Escalation (`#430`, [#436](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/436) `714c182`):** `guardrail_offer.evaluate` Rule 6 verifies `offer.journey_id == journey.journey_id` (`journey_mismatch`), and `CustomerSkills._deliver` in `customer/skills.py` suppresses the `hotel_voucher` attachment and text when `rec.hotel is None`. Weak live evidence on `acme-irrops-demo-00271-8k2` (`hotel-unavailable` has `hotel = null`, `vouchers = [meal, meal]`, and no WhatsApp message carrying a `hotel_voucher`); Rule 6 `journey_mismatch` is not reachable through the API and is covered by unit tests (`173 passed`, policy branch coverage `100%`).
- **Live DCS Seat Inventory & Refused Conversion Logging (`#354`, [#373](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/373) `c71add8`; `#380`, `#381`, [#384](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/384) `a565f16`):** `SimWorld.cabin_inventory()` caps `open_seats` by unheld seats in `Flight.open_seats` and `dcs_state(self).holds`; `conversion._convert()` releases the held DCS seat (`_release_held_seat`) on every post-hold failure path (`payments.charge` error, post-charge guardrail rejection, `seat.assign` error) and records `GuardrailDecision(False, reasons)` on refused conversions. Verified live on `acme-irrops-demo-00227-h4f`.
- **Sales Radar Post-Proposal Recording (`#392`, [#405](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/405) `6c6a70a`):** `SalesRadar.tick()` records `(journey_id, run_id, flight)` in `self._sent` only after `await self._customer.propose_offer(offer)` succeeds, allowing the next tick to retry transient Customer RPC errors. Verified live on `acme-sales-agent` (`1985603925432074240`).
- **Suppression on Open Requests & Live Offer Invalidation on `needs_human` (`#325`, [#410](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/410) `7652d12`):** `ineligible_reason(rec)` returns `"open_request_pending"` whenever `state.open_requests` is non-empty, `journey_status` sets `resolution_acknowledged = state.resolution_acknowledged and not state.open_requests`, and `JourneyState.append_event` invalidates live offers when `needs_human` is recorded. Verified live on `acme-irrops-demo-00245-z2q`.
- **ANAC 400 `DOC-05..08` Duty of Care & Wait Calculation (`#396`, [#407](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/407) `0f24980`; `#324`, [#415](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/415) `6484858`):** `rights.assess()` measures passenger wait from `min(detected_at, original_departure)` (`10.2h` for Mariana's 22:03 detection vs 08:15 rebook), and `duty.py`, `concierge.py`, `outreach.py`, and `verify.py` enforce `DOC-05..08` (`origin_resident` ground transfer without hotel, `special_needs` daytime hotel + transfer, and meal voucher verification). Verified live on `acme-irrops-demo-00245-z2q`.
- **Replanning Escalation Reply & Hotel Pickup Rescheduling (`#398`, [#412](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/412) `f81214a`):** `ReplanningChangeAgent` sends `ESCALATION_REPLY` on WhatsApp whenever rebooking or a downstream specialist step fails, books the rescheduled airport transfer from the booked hotel (`hotel_id`), and cancels the superseded transfer only after the replacement succeeds. Verified live on `acme-irrops-demo-00245-z2q`.
- **Connection Feasibility Uses `World.mct_minutes` (`#414`, [#419](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/419) `559003c`):** Threads `self.world.mct_minutes` through `runtime.py`, `IrropsRebooking`, `rebuild_journey`, and `ReplanningChangeAgent` instead of the hardcoded 90-minute default. Verified live on `acme-irrops-demo-00245-z2q`.
- **Zero-Bag & Economy Seat Verification (`#416`, [#418](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/418) `5f47dab`):** `verify_journey` passes `baggage` verification when `pnr["bags"] == []` and requires `cabin == "economy"` on un-upgraded seat assignments. Verified live on `acme-irrops-demo-00245-z2q`.
- **Deterministic Veto on LLM `Acknowledge` & Delimiter Stripping (`#421`, [#427](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/427) `80685c6`):** Applies the deterministic negation/question/time-request veto (`_VETO`, `_TIME`, `"?"`) even when `VertexLlm` classifies a message as `Acknowledge`, and strips `<passenger_text>` delimiters iteratively until fixed point. Verified in CI (`test_intent.py`).
- **Outreach Stale-Facts Metric & Case-Insensitive Fact Matching (`#422`, [#425](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/425) `72e85a8`):** `send_notification` records `metrics.template_fallback("resolution")` when post-LLM facts drift, and `_contains_all` matches word-based required facts case-insensitively while keeping flight/seat/hotel/voucher identifiers exact. Verified in CI (`test_outreach.py`, `test_offer_delivery.py`).
- **Recovery Quote Cost-Floor Guard & `current_flight` Sync (`#446`, [#450](https://github.com/duboc/acme-airlines/pull/450) `f018ff0`):** `recovery_quote` returns `None` when `inventory.open_seats <= 0` or `inventory.cost_floor >= inventory.standard_upgrade_fare`, `SalesRadar._price` rejects non-discounted or negative-margin quotes, and `rebuild_journey`, `ReplanningChangeAgent.handle`, and `CustomerSkills.journey_status` synchronize `state.current_flight` across replanning. Verified in CI (`215 passed`, policy branch coverage `100%`).

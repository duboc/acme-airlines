- **Release:** `v0.1.0` (MVP Release)
- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Scope:** Autonomous Disruption Resolution & Guardrailed Upsell Factory Deliverable
- **Core Governance Rule:** "Two Goals, One Guardrail" (Service advocacy strictly outranks revenue)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The ACME Airlines software factory has completed and verified the **v0.1.0 MVP** of the proactive flight disruption (IRROPS) resolution and guardrailed ancillary upselling platform.

Built around the operational scenario of **Mariana Silva (Diamond Frequent Flyer)** whose flight **AC1234 (GRU → SCL)** is cancelled due to unscheduled maintenance, the system autonomously detects disruptions backstage, resolves the itinerary across airline and partner systems, fulfills regulatory duty-of-care, conducts empathetic passenger communications over simulated WhatsApp, handles conversational replanning, and executes contextual, margin-optimized upgrades under strict policy guardrails.

The system is fully demonstrated via a TypeScript/React **Operator Console** and a **Simulated WhatsApp Passenger Client**, backed by an autonomous multi-agent architecture powered by Google ADK and Gemini on Vertex AI.

## 2. What the MVP Demonstrates: Scenarios 1 through 5 & Governance

The MVP implements the five end-to-end operational scenarios defined in the product specification, strictly bounded by the **"Two Goals, One Guardrail"** principle:

```
[Operational Event: OCC Cancellation]
                 │
                 ▼
     [ACME Customer Agent] ── Dedicated VM runtime (Google Cloud / Gemini)
                 │
  ┌──────────────┼──────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┐
  ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼              ▼
[IRROPS]      [Seat]       [Baggage]      [Rights]       [Concierge]
Rebooking     14A Hold     WorldTracer    Duty-of-Care   Hotel/Voucher
  └──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┘
                                │
                 [Proactive WhatsApp Outreach]
                                │
     [Customer Dynamic Preference: Departure > 10:00 AM]
                                │
     [Itinerary Adjusted: AC1252 @ 11:40 AM + Connection Kept]
                                │
                 [Sales Agent: Margin Radar]
                                │ (A2A pitch: $135 upgrade)
                                ▼
               [Customer Agent Guardrail Check]
                                │ (Approved: genuine courtesy)
                                ▼
            [WhatsApp Delivery + 1-Tap Passenger Acceptance]
                                │
         [Seat 2A Lie-Flat Confirmed ── +$80 Net Margin]
```

### Scenario 1 — Silent Ingestion & Autonomous Profile Evaluation
- **Trigger:** OCC publishes cancellation for flight `AC1234` (São Paulo GRU → Santiago SCL) at 22:03 due to unscheduled maintenance.
- **Quiet-Hours Backstage Activation:** The Customer agent listens on the flight ops event stream. Outside business hours (22:00–07:00), the system operates silently backstage without alarming the sleeping passenger.
- **Context Loading:** Ingests Mariana's CRM profile, active PNR `MSLV42`, Diamond tier status, preferred window seat `14A`, 1 checked bag (`BAG0001`), and identifies the critical onward connection in Santiago (`AC0800` at 14:00 the following day).

### Scenario 2 — Zero-Touch Backstage Journey Rebuilding & Duty-of-Care
- **Autonomous Rebooking:** The Customer Agent coordinates with the IRROPS Rebooking specialist to identify and book `AC1240` (08:15 AM nonstop departure), protecting the 14:00 Santiago connection with 225 minutes of buffer.
- **Seat & Baggage Continuity:** Seat specialist assigns courtesy window seat `14A` on the new aircraft; Baggage specialist invokes WorldTracer to retag and route checked bag `BAG0001` to `AC1240`.
- **Regulatory Duty-of-Care:** Rights & Compensation specialist assesses entitlements under regulatory frameworks; Concierge specialist books partner hotel **#HT8842** (`HB-AD0362EF`), schedules airport ground transfers, and generates digital meal/dinner vouchers.
- **Sales Suppression:** The Sales Agent margin radar detects the disruption, but Customer Agent governance strictly blocks any commercial pitches during unacknowledged disruption recovery (`sales_suppressed` logged).

### Scenario 3 — Proactive Notification & Conversational Replanning
- **Consolidated Outreach:** At 22:09 (6-minute quiet-hours delay), passenger receives a single, empathetic WhatsApp message containing the complete resolution: cancellation notice, new flight `AC1240`, seat `14A`, hotel `HT8842`, transfers, and vouchers.
- **Conversational Dynamic Replanning:** Mariana responds: *"Can I leave after 10am?"* The Change Agent specialist parses the constraint (`DepartureAfter(10:00)`), identifies `AC1252` departing at 11:40 AM, verifies the Santiago connection remains intact, preserves seat `14A`, retags baggage, reschedules hotel-to-airport transfers, and confirms the update in seconds.
- **Infeasible Replanning Protection:** If the passenger asks for an infeasible departure (e.g. *"after 2pm"*, which would miss the 14:00 Santiago connection), the agent provides an honest explanation and safely keeps the passenger confirmed on `AC1240`.

### Scenario 4 — Inter-Agent Negotiation & Guardrailed Upsell
- **A2A Margin Radar:** With the disruption resolved and acknowledged, Sales Agent scans cabin inventory on `AC1252`, detecting 4 open lie-flat seats (`2A`, `2B`, `3A`, `3B`) in Premium Business.
- **Dynamic Pricing:** `margin_opt` calculates a recovery upgrade price of **$135.00** (standard $290.00), securing **+$80.00 net margin**.
- **Deterministic 6-Rule Guardrail Check:** Sales submits a structured `Offer` to Customer Agent over A2A (`POST /a2a`). Customer Agent validates the offer against policy:
  1. `disruption_resolved`: Core journey is 100% resolved.
  2. `resolution_acknowledged`: Passenger has acknowledged the recovery.
  3. `genuine_discount`: Flight and cabin open; price is below standard fare and within Diamond tier cap.
  4. `margin_verified`: Price exceeds cost floor with positive net margin.
  5. `zero_pressure`: Optional courtesy; no duplicate offers allowed.
  6. `origin_and_flight_verified`: Sourced from Sales; matches active flight.
- **Zero-Pressure Delivery:** Delivered to Mariana's WhatsApp chat with explicit "Optional. You can ignore this." phrasing and distinct **Accept** and **No thanks** actions.
- **Offer Decline Path:** Selecting "No thanks" immediately transitions the offer to `declined`, runs closed-loop verification, records `offer_declined`, closes the journey with $0 charged, and never nudges again.

### Scenario 5 — Frictionless Conversion & Closed-Loop Verification
- **One-Tap Conversion:** Mariana taps **Accept** in WhatsApp. Under per-journey locking, Customer Agent executes: `dcs:seat.hold` → `payments:charge` ($135.00 with idempotency key) → post-charge guardrail re-check → `dcs:seat.assign` (`2A`) → `dcs:seat.release` (`14A`) → digital boarding pass issuance.
- **Idempotency & Double-Tap Defense:** Rapid repeated taps are deduplicated by the UI and verified via payment idempotency keys; exactly one charge is processed.
- **Compensating Transactions:** Any downstream failure after charging automatically voids the payment (`payments:charge.void`).
- **Closed-Loop Verification:** Verification specialist validates consistency across 5 systems: PSS flight booking, DCS seat assignment (`2A`), WorldTracer baggage routing, Concierge hotel booking, and Payments ledger ($135.00 net charge).
- **Journey Closure:** Interaction logged to CRM, NPS survey scheduled (+24h), journey memory committed to persistent SQLite Memory Bank, and journey status transitioned to `CLOSED`.

## 3. Architecture & Governance Blueprint

The implementation demonstrates strict adherence to the **"Two Goals, One Guardrail"** governance framework:

1. **Physical & Network Isolation:**
   - Sales Agent runs as an independent service (`acme-sales`) with no access to passenger messaging channels (WhatsApp), payments, PSS, DCS, CRM, or database backends.
   - Simulator access control (`acme-sim`) enforces a deny-by-default allowlist: caller `sales` is authorized **only** to access the `inventory` MCP server.
2. **PII-Free Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Surface:**
   - Inter-agent communication over `POST /a2a` uses Google ADK / JSON-RPC with frozen, strictly-typed schemas (`extra="forbid"`).
   - Skills exposed: `list_journeys` (returns anonymous journey IDs), `journey_status` (returns exactly 4 non-PII fields: `status`, `flight`, `resolution_acknowledged`, `run_id`), and `propose_offer`.
   - Sales prices offers assuming courtesy tier `diamond` without knowing passenger identity; Customer Agent authoritatively verifies eligibility against real CRM data.
3. **Concurrency & Race Resistance:**
   - Atomic per-journey locking (`CustomerRuntime.journey_lock`) prevents time-of-check/time-of-use (TOCTOU) races between offer delivery, inbound passenger replanning requests, and conversion taps.
   - Dual guardrail evaluation: re-verified immediately before sending WhatsApp outreach and re-verified post-charge before final seat assignment.
4. **Prompt Injection Hardening:**
   - Inbound message parsing maps passenger text into a closed discriminated union (`DepartureAfter`, `Acknowledge`, `Other`). Unrecognized inputs trigger operator escalation (`needs_human`).
   - Outbound LLM composition operates exclusively on Customer-owned state facts. Sales text or metadata cannot influence generated messages.

## 4. How to Run the Application

### 4.1 Prerequisites
- [Docker](https://docs.docker.com/get-docker/) & Docker Compose
- Alternatively, for bare-metal local execution:
  - Python 3.12+ with [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/)
  - Node.js 20+ with npm

### 4.2 Running Full Stack with Docker Compose (Recommended)

Generate bearer tokens and start the 4-service stack:

```bash
# 1. Export simulator tokens and sales credential
export ACME_SIM_TOKENS="customer=$(openssl rand -hex 24),sales=$(openssl rand -hex 24),test-admin=$(openssl rand -hex 24)"
export ACME_SALES_TOKEN=$(echo "$ACME_SIM_TOKENS" | sed -n 's/.*sales=\([^,]*\).*/\1/p')

# 2. Start all services (offline/deterministic mode, $0 cost)
docker compose -f app/compose.yaml up --build
```

#### Running in Live Vertex AI Mode
To run the live demo with Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI (requires authenticated Google ADC on the host):

```bash
LLM_MODE=vertex docker compose -f app/compose.yaml up --build
```

*Note:* Vertex AI operates exclusively with ADC, project `acme-airlines-factory`, and endpoint `locations/global`. No API keys or regional endpoints are permitted.

#### Accessing Web Interfaces
- **Operator Console:** [http://localhost:8080/console](http://localhost:8080/console) (or port `5173` if running dev server)
- **Simulated WhatsApp Chat:** [http://localhost:8080/whatsapp](http://localhost:8080/whatsapp)
- **Runtime REST/SSE API Docs:** [http://localhost:8000/docs](http://localhost:8000/docs) (or internal port `8800`)

### 4.3 Running Components Locally (Development Mode)

#### Backend Runtime & Simulators
```bash
cd app/backend
uv sync --frozen

# Lint and unit/governance tests
uv run ruff check
uv run ruff format --check
uv run pytest -m "not live"

# Start services individually
uv run acme-sim       # Starts simulated MCP enterprise backends (port 8001)
uv run acme-customer  # Starts Customer Agent runtime & REST/SSE API (port 8800)
uv run acme-sales     # Starts autonomous Sales Agent A2A margin radar (port 8801)
```

#### Frontend Client
```bash
cd app/frontend
npm ci
npm run lint          # ESLint & Prettier
npm test              # Vitest unit & component tests
npm run build         # TypeScript build & Vite bundle
npm run dev           # Start Vite dev server on http://localhost:5173
```

#### End-to-End Playwright Suite
```bash
cd app/frontend
npm run e2e           # Full Playwright narrative browser test suite
```

## 6. Known Limitations & Post-MVP Backlog

### Post-MVP Architecture Issues
The following deployment items are documented in `docs/product/deploy.md` and tracked as post-MVP backlog issues:

1. **Issue #100 — Cloud Run: Send ID token in `X-Serverless-Authorization` from HTTP connectors:**
   - In Cloud Run service-to-service communication, Google IAM requires an ID token. Because `Authorization: Bearer <token>` carries the internal simulator bearer token, Cloud Run deployments must send the GCP ID token in `X-Serverless-Authorization`. Blocked on product-owner decision to deploy to Cloud Run.
2. **Issue #101 — Cloud Run: App-level caller identity check on `/a2a`:**
   - IAM authentication on Cloud Run validates caller identity at the service perimeter, not per route. While nginx routing isolates `/a2a`, application-level validation should explicitly verify that the calling service account is `sales-agent`. Blocked on product-owner decision to deploy to Cloud Run.
3. **Issue #102 — Split Deployment: Replace in-process simulators with `acme-sim` HTTP calls:**
   - For rapid local development and testing, `acme-customer` mounts simulators in-process. In a split multi-container Cloud Run deployment, the Customer runtime must communicate with `acme-sim` exclusively over HTTP with bearer tokens. Blocked on product-owner decision to deploy to Cloud Run.

### Security Audit Findings & Resolved Vulnerabilities (`docs/security/audit-m4.md`)
The Milestone 4 Security Audit passed with **0 vulnerabilities**. Four pre-audit historical vulnerabilities were identified and resolved during development:
- **Issue #83 (Fixed in PR #85):** `propose_offer` accepted proposals outside `RESOLVED`; now rejects them with `disruption_not_resolved` and records nothing.
- **Issue #88 (Fixed in PR #93):** `accept_offer` TOCTOU race between check and charge; fixed by locking `handle_inbound` and adding a post-charge guardrail re-check with payment voiding.
- **Issue #87 (Fixed in PR #89):** Economy hold on 14A remained active after upgrading to 2A; resolved with DCS `seat.release` on seat assignment.
- **Issue #97 (Fixed in PR #99):** Duplicate proactive WhatsApp notification under concurrent `tick()`; resolved with atomic check-and-send under `journey_lock`.

### Exploratory Acceptance Findings (`docs/qa/acceptance-mvp.md`)
Exploratory testing validated all 5 golden path scenarios with photographic evidence. Three defects were filed and immediately resolved:
- **Issue #104 (Fixed in PR #109):** SalesRadar sent-set retained proposals across scenario resets; fixed with opaque per-run nonce `run_id`.
- **Issue #105 (Fixed in PR #110):** Operations console lost active journey state on page refresh; fixed by restoring journey on mount and re-attaching SSE stream.
- **Issue #106 (Fixed in PR #108):** Frontend JourneyCard omitted partner hotel property code; fixed by rendering `HT8842`.
